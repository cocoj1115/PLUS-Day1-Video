# PLUS Intro Video — Storyboard

The shot list for the film as built (v4). Times are film times; every reveal is cued to the
narration's word timings in `assets/voice/vo_meta.json`.

- **Length:** 1:51 (voiceover 1:48 + 0.3 s lead-in + ~2.8 s closing hold)
- **Format:** 1920×1080, 30 fps, hand-drawn style from the PLUS Teacher Handbook (`design/brand.md`)
- **Voice:** HeyGen "Cassidy – Expressive & Imaginative"
- **Audio:** voiceover + small sound effects on on-screen actions. No music bed; a short chime at the logo and at "You've got this!".

## Cast

| Role | Played by |
|---|---|
| You (the student) | Pink **Plus** mascot |
| Your tutor | Blue **Equals** mascot, usually inside a video-call tile |
| Real tutors | The PLUS team photo with the Carnegie Mellon University wordmark |
| Teacher | Yellow **Divide** mascot |
| Other students | Green **Greater-than**, coral flower, cyan wave mascots |

## Visual rules

- One continuous world: the hill landscape runs through the whole film; between scenes the camera slides **left** along it.
- Zoom only for meaning: the camera pushes **into** the Zoom window (scene 3 → 4, a matched cut) and pulls **back** to the rooms map (scene 4).
- Every item appears when the voice names it. On-screen text is short labels only.
- Zoom UI is drawn in the handbook style, not screenshots. Doodles are rough.js lines with a crayon grain (`tools/node/doodles.mjs`).
