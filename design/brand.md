# PLUS Video — Visual Style Guide

Source: Figma **PLUS Teacher Handbook** (“PLUS Teacher Field Guide — Prototype 14”).
There is no published design system; this guide is extracted from the handbook so the video matches it.
Tokens live in `design/tokens.css`. Imported assets live in `.media/images/` (tracked in `.media/manifest.jsonl`).

## Look & feel
- Warm, friendly, hand-made: **cream paper background**, deep-teal headings, soft pastel cards.
- **Crayon-texture math-symbol characters** with simple line-art arms/legs — the PLUS mascots.
- Hand-drawn touches: pencil-outline cards, highlighter swipes behind titles, sketchy grass.
- Font: **DM Sans** only (900/800 headings, 700 labels, 400–500 body).

## Rules for the video
- Background cream `#F9F5EF`; cards white or pastel with pencil-style outline.
- Headings deep teal, DM Sans 800–900; step labels blue, uppercase.
- Use the characters as guides/hosts; animate them with gentle, purposeful moves (no idle wobble).
- Use the PLUS logo (icon + wordmark) at open and close only.
- Script sections → color coding suggestion: Zoom/setup = blue, tutor/working = yellow, goals = green, help/problems = coral.
