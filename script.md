# PLUS Intro Video — Script

- **Audience:** Grades 5–8 (middle school)
- **Length:** 330 spoken words ≈ 2:12 at 150 wpm (~2:20–2:30 with pauses)
- **Tone:** friendly, direct, short sentences, no jargon
- **Visuals:** Figma design system (link: _TODO_)

> VO = 旁白原文（逐字，勿改）。On screen = 画面建议，可随时改。

---

## 1. Welcome (0:00–0:08)
**VO:**
Welcome to PLUS! We're excited to meet you.

PLUS helps you learn math with support from an online tutor.

**On screen:** PLUS logo + 欢迎标题；学生 + 笔记本电脑 + 数学图标

## 2. Who is your tutor? (0:08–0:26)
**VO:**
So, who is your tutor?

Your tutor is a college student, usually from Carnegie Mellon University. They'll get to know you, answer your questions, and help you work through tricky problems. It's okay to make mistakes or say, "I don't understand yet." That's part of learning!

**On screen:** 大学生 tutor 形象（CMU）；对话气泡 "I don't understand yet." 👍

## 3. Getting started: Zoom + math software (0:26–0:43)
**VO:**
Here's how to get started.

First, put on your headphones and join Zoom using the link your teacher gives you. Open your math software, too. Keep both open during tutoring.

You'll start in the main Zoom room, where the tutors will welcome you.

**On screen:** 步骤 1：耳机 → Zoom 链接 → 数学软件，两个窗口并排；主会议室里多位 tutor 挥手

## 4. Your breakout room (0:43–1:07)
**VO:**
Next, your tutor will move you into your own breakout room—a smaller Zoom room just for you.

You might only see yourself at first. That's okay! You're in the right place. Your tutor helps a few students and visits their rooms one at a time. Stay in your room and start your math work. Your tutor will come to you.

**On screen:** 步骤 2：大房间 → 小房间动画；只有自己的画面 + "You're in the right place ✓"；tutor 依次访问几个房间的示意图

## 5. When your tutor joins (1:07–1:21)
**VO:**
When your tutor joins, say hello! Make sure you can hear each other. Then share your screen so they can see your math. Show them what you've tried, and tell them where you need help.

**On screen:** 步骤 3：打招呼 → 🔊 检查声音 → "Share Screen" 按钮高亮 → 屏幕上的数学题

## 6. Math goals + fruit snack (1:21–1:38)
**VO:**
Your tutor will also help you set math goals, like how much time you'll spend practicing or how much work you'll finish. Each week, if you reach at least one of your goals, you can earn a fruit snack from your teacher!

**On screen:** 目标卡片（⏱ 练习时间 / ✅ 完成题量）；达成目标 → 水果软糖奖励 🎉

## 7. When your tutor steps out (1:38–2:02)
**VO:**
Sometimes, your tutor will step out to help another student. Stay in your room and keep working. They'll check back in with you.

Need help before they return? Click "Raise Hand" in Zoom. While you wait, try another step or another problem if you can.

If you can't hear your tutor or can't share your screen, let your teacher know.

**On screen:** tutor 离开 → 学生继续做题；"Raise Hand" ✋ 按钮高亮；技术问题 → 找老师图标

## 8. Outro (2:02–2:12)
**VO:**
Keep practicing, ask questions, and take it one step at a time. Your tutor is here to help you reach your goals.

You've got this!

**On screen:** 回顾三个关键词（Practice · Ask · One step at a time）；"You've got this!" + PLUS logo
